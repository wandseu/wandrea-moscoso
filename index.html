Hello!

It's Wandrea!

I'm a UI/UX designer and front-end developer with 8+ years across maritime crewing platforms, financial planning tools and internal business systems. I do the requirements gathering, the sitemaps and flows, the interface — then build enough of the front-end in Razor, JavaScript and React that handover is a pull request instead of a spec.

How I work: something clickable in days, not weeks. I use Figma Make and Claude Design to bulk out states, copy variants and throwaway prototypes so stakeholders react to a product early. The judgement stays mine — the tedious middle gets automated.

Away from the screen I like a little adventure — but once the battery drains I'm a full home buddy. Cats and games, mostly.
